Privacy policy.

This page explains the basic privacy practices for escalivo.com. Escalivo only asks for information needed to respond to enquiries, assess fit, or provide agreed services.

Information you provide

When you book a call, send an email, or otherwise contact Escalivo, you may provide your name, business contact details, company information, and information about your sales or marketing needs. Booking providers process the details you submit under their own privacy terms.

How information is used

  • To respond to enquiries and arrange strategy calls.
  • To assess whether Escalivo's services are a fit.
  • To deliver and improve services when an engagement is agreed.
  • To meet legal, security, and record-keeping obligations.

Sharing and retention

Information is not sold. It may be shared with service providers that support scheduling, communication, infrastructure, and contracted work, or where disclosure is legally required. Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for these purposes.

Your choices

To ask about, correct, or request deletion of personal information held by Escalivo, email hello@escalivo.com.
