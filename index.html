FAQ

Common concerns.
Clear answers.

Who is Escalivo built for?

B2B SaaS founders with a validated product, a definable market, and the capacity to take more sales conversations. If you are still searching for product–market fit, the strategy call will tell you quickly whether outbound is premature.

What makes a demo “qualified”?

A meeting should only count when the prospect matches the buyer criteria agreed before launch and books a conversation with your team. Fit is defined around the role, company profile, segment, and buying context—not just an email reply.

What does the nine-step system include?

It covers ICP and offer alignment, signal discovery, data and enrichment, audience segmentation, channel and sequence design, campaign execution, conversation management, meeting routing, and continuous optimization.

How does the 120-day offer work?

Escalivo’s public offer is 1,000 qualified demos in 120 days or you don’t pay. Exact scope, qualification criteria, dependencies, and commercial terms are confirmed in writing before work begins.

How soon can campaigns go live?

The first priority is getting the market, offer, data, and sending system right. Launch timing depends on your market and current infrastructure; the strategy call is where Escalivo maps the fastest responsible path.

What happens on the strategy call?

You will look at your ICP, offer, current channels, and the point where growth is getting stuck. The goal is a useful diagnosis and a clear next test—not a generic agency pitch deck.
